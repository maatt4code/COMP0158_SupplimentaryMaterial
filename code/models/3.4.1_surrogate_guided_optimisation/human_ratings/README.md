# Human ratings — none, and that is the finding

§3.4.1 is the automated loop. Nothing here was rated by a listener: the reward
signal is a distilled proxy trained on frozen MERT and audEERING scores. The
absence of rating data in this directory is the point of §4.1.

The correlations reported against human judgement (ρ = −0.164 and +0.881) were
not produced here. They come from reading the resulting parameters back through
the θ-KRR model fitted in §3.4.2, so they belong to that directory's data.
